    What is SMART NFTs?

    A self-custodial wallet for publishing content as collectible editions on Bitcoin SV. You publish something once; anyone with your link can buy their own copy in a single click. The rules — your publisher fee and the resale split — are enforced by the network itself. There's no account and no server: your wallet lives in this browser, everything else lives on-chain.

    Quick start

    1. Back up your wallet. In Wallet, reveal your 12-word seed phrase and write it down somewhere safe — it restores everything on any device. (A raw WIF is also shown as an equivalent backup.)
    2. Add a little BSV. Send a small amount to your address (shown in Wallet) — you need it to publish and to buy.
    3. Publish an edition. In Publish, set a name, cover, description, an optional file, and a price model, then mint.
    4. Share it. Your edition appears in My NFTs — click Sales page to open its public link, then Share it anywhere.
    5. Get paid. Anyone who opens the link presses Get a copy and owns one instantly; you (the publisher) and the current holder are paid automatically, on-chain.

    Using each tab

    💰 Wallet

    Your keys, balance, and backup.

    1. Check your balance — click Refresh balance.
    2. Receive BSV — send funds to your Address (you need a little to publish or buy).
    3. Share your identity — Copy public key to give to anyone sending you NFTs or messages.
    4. Back up — reveal your 12-word seed phrase, write it down, keep it secret. Anyone with it controls the wallet; lose it with no backup and the wallet can't be recovered. (The WIF is shown too as an equivalent backup.)
    5. Restore / recover — enter your words in Restore from seed phrase (or a WIF in Restore from WIF) → Restore; your holdings and purchases rebuild from chain.
    6. New wallet — makes a fresh wallet and shows its new seed phrase to write down (back up the current one first — it gets replaced). Note: wallets imported from a WIF have no seed phrase.

    📤 Publish

    Create editions of your content for anyone to buy.

    1. Enter a collection name and description, and add a cover image (what buyers see).
    2. Pick a pricing model: Fixed fees (flat publisher + holder fee in sats, min 1 each), or Percentage (publisher takes a % of a reseller-set price).
    3. Set the bond per edition — a small refundable deposit (default 2100 sats) that rides with each copy and is reclaimable by burning. Gives every copy a price floor; pick a low bond for free drops, higher for premium.
    4. Set how many editions to mint now, and optionally embed a file — tick Encrypt to make it holders-only.
    5. Click Mint edition collection (needs a little BSV). Your new editions appear in My NFTs.

    How selling works

    Editions are "unlimited mints": a buyer doesn't take your copy — they mint their own copy from yours, permissionlessly, while you keep yours. Each copy can then be a source for the next buyer. Two price models:

    • Fixed fees — a flat publisher fee + holder fee (in satoshis), paid on every copy.
    • Percentage — the publisher takes a fixed % of the sale price; the reseller keeps the rest. The split is computed and enforced by the covenant on every sale.

    Backups & recovery

    Your only secret is your WIF. If you lose this browser's data, paste the WIF into Wallet → Restore from WIF on any device to rebuild your holdings and purchases straight from the blockchain. If you lose the WIF with no backup, the wallet can't be recovered — so back it up.

    Air-gapped (cold) signing

    Keep your private key on a permanently-offline machine and still transact. Only files cross the gap — the key never does. It works for plain BSV payments and edition transfers / burns. Two machines:

    • Offline (cold) box — a device with no network that holds your wallet (its seed phrase / WIF). It only signs.
    • Online (watch) box — an internet-connected device that holds no key. It views your holdings, exports unsigned requests, and broadcasts signed ones.

    One-time setup: on the offline box, open the app and create/restore your wallet, then copy its public key (Wallet tab — it's not a secret). On the online box, go to Wallet → 👁 Watch a wallet, paste that public key, and tap Watch. The online box now shows your balance and holdings with every signing button hidden.

    To send (the 3-step loop):

    1. Export (online box). Fill in ⚡ Send BSV (or pick an edition under ⚙ Advanced — air-gapped signing) and tap Export for offline signing →. It downloads a …airgap-request.json file. Move it to the offline box (USB stick).
    2. Sign (offline box). Open Wallet → ⚙ Advanced → 2 · Sign offline, import the request file, check the summary, and tap Sign & export signed tx →. It downloads a signed-tx file. Move that back to the online box.
    3. Broadcast (online box). Open ⚙ Advanced → 3 · Broadcast signed, import (or paste) the signed file, and tap Broadcast →. Done — the payment is on-chain.

    The signer only ever produces the exact transaction the online wallet described, and refuses anything its key doesn't own. Use a fresh, dedicated USB, and never type your seed phrase / WIF into an online device. Full guide: docs/AIR_GAPPED.md.

    FAQ

    Do buyers need an account?No — anyone with a funded wallet buys from a link in one click. No sign-up, no server.
    What does a copy cost?The price the publisher set, plus a tiny network (miner) fee. The Sales page shows the exact split before you confirm.
    Is my file private?Tick Encrypt when publishing and only holders can open it. It's an inconvenience to non-holders, not hard DRM.
    What's the bond?A small refundable deposit that rides with each edition (default 2100 sats). It gives every copy a price floor and is reclaimed by burning the copy — it's not a fee, nobody keeps it.
    Can I see who bought my work?Yes — 👥 Buyers on a collection, or the 📊 Sales tab for everything at once, and you can DM them. It's buyers at point of sale; onward transfers aren't visible to you.
    What are Threads?A members-only discussion scoped to a collection's lineage — you see the creator and everyone up/down your line, with un-spoofable creator/holder badges.
    Are my contacts safe if I switch devices?Use ☁ Back up in 📇 Contacts. It's encrypted to your own key and auto-restores when you Restore from WIF.
    Can I keep my key offline?Yes — see Air-gapped (cold) signing above. The online device runs watch-only (no key); a permanently-offline device signs. Only files cross between them.
    Can I earn when buyers fund their wallet?Yes. In Wallet → 💰 Earn referrals, save your SimpleSwap referral link. It rides on every sales page you share, so buyers who tap 💵 Buy BSV get BSV under your code and you earn the commission.
    Why does it say experimental?The covenant tech is new and runs on real mainnet BSV — use small amounts while it's being proven.
